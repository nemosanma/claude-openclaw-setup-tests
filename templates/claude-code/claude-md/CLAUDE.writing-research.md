# Project: research & writing workspace (copy to ./CLAUDE.md)

This folder is for research briefs, drafts, and notes — not software.

## Folder layout
- `inbox/` raw material (pasted articles, exports). Read-only.
- `notes/` one Markdown file per topic, named `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
- `briefs/` finished research briefs (use the `research-brief` skill).
- `drafts/` long-form writing in progress.

## Rules
- Every factual claim in a brief gets a source link. If you cannot find one, mark it **[unverified]**.
- Quote at most two sentences from any single source; summarize the rest in your own words.
- Never invent statistics, quotes, or URLs.
- Dates: write them as `2026-10-29` and say which time zone if a time matters.
- Do not give financial, legal, or medical advice; flag such questions for a professional.

## Style
- Plain English, short paragraphs, active voice.
- Lead with the answer, then the evidence.
